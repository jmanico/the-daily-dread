payments-api may use a vulnerable version of example-xml-parser

Completed with gaps Critical, source CVSS 3.1 Exploitation reported Business impact unknown
62Medium

The lockfile pins 2.4.1, inside the affected range. Reachability and deployment are not established, and Dependabot alerts could not be read.

A version match is not proof of exploitation or compromise. Analyzed 4 Nov, 07:06, report version 3.

What could go wrong

Analysis

If payments-api parses XML from outside the company with this library, an attacker could make it read local files or reach internal services.

Protections that may reduce risk

  • Verified in configThe service reads XML only in src/import/statement.ts, behind an admin-only route.
  • InferredThe deployment manifest declares no outbound network access. Repository configuration does not prove the live deployment matches.

What to do next

  1. Upgrade example-xml-parser to 2.4.3 or later.
    Why
    2.4.3 disables external entities by default, per the advisory.
    Needs
    http-kit must accept 2.4.3. Its own range allows it.
    Verify
    The lockfile shows 2.4.3 or later on every listed branch.
  2. Confirm who can reach the statement import route.
    Why
    Reachability decides whether this is exploitable at all.
    Needs
    Access to the live route configuration.
    Verify
    Record the result as a company note. Confidence updates on the next advisory event.

Critique notes

Screenshots were taken at 390 px and 1440 px and reviewed against the brief: does the screen say how sure we are, and what is missing?

  • At 1440 px the report stopped at 1080 px while the header ran to 1280 px. The narrative column now fills the page and the evidence rail grew to 380 px. Prose keeps its 68-character measure.
  • Confidence tracks started at different x positions depending on the score width. The score column is now fixed, so meters line up.
  • Coverage gaps were framed with 3 px of hatching, which read as a dashed border, not as "unknown". The frame is now 6 px, so the signature reads as hatching.
  • At 390 px the spec sheet lost its gutters because the sheet's padding overrode the page wrapper. It now sets only vertical padding.
  • The verdict headline broke inside the package name. It is now capped at 30 characters, so it wraps between words.
  • Dependency paths broke mid-name in the narrow rail. Identifiers now wrap at spaces first.
  • One decoration removed: the 4 px left stripe on error panels. The heading, wash, and border already identify the error.
